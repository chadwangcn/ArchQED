# ArchQED Principles

1. Human architecture is the source of intent.
2. Machine contracts are derived and replaceable artifacts.
3. Architecture change and feature refinement have different blast radii.
4. Backend tooling is discovered from evidence or configured by humans, never invented.
5. Project-specific business knowledge does not belong in the ArchQED core.
6. Ambiguity blocks execution instead of inviting plausible guesses.
7. Implementation and verification are separate roles.
8. Completion is computed from reproducible evidence.
9. No evidence, no done.

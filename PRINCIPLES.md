# ArchQED Principles

ArchQED separates four planes: human intent, generated control data, product execution, and verification evidence. Structural architecture changes receive conservative broad impact. Feature refinements may remain local only when stable IDs and traceability prove the boundary. Uncertainty expands impact rather than inventing precision. States such as `needs_clarification`, `needs_recompile`, and `implemented_unverified` are engineering facts, not failures to hide.
